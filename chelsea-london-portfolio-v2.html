about

A decade in product,
now on camera.

I've spent ten years working as a product owner in tech — which means I've sat in the exact meetings where a feature gets built, and then watched teams struggle to explain it to actual humans.

Along the way I wrote the technical documentation and produced the training videos that were supposed to fix that gap. Turns out I liked that part the most.

So now I make content that does the same thing, just outside a company handbook — clear, human, and a little less corporate.
